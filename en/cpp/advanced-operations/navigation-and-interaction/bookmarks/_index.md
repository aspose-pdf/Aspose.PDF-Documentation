Bookmarks form a document outline tree. An outline entry can define a page destination or action, and parent items can contain child bookmarks.

- [Add and Delete a Bookmark](/pdf/cpp/add-and-delete-bookmark/) - Create bookmark entries and hierarchies or remove existing bookmarks.
- [Get, Update and Expand a Bookmark](/pdf/cpp/get-update-and-expand-bookmark/) - Inspect bookmark properties and page targets, update a child, and expand top-level entries.
