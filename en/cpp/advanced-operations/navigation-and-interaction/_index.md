Use links to connect pages and external resources, bookmarks to organize document navigation, and actions to attach behavior to document events and form controls. Action execution depends on the PDF viewer; generating a file does not execute its embedded actions.

- [Links](/pdf/cpp/links/) - Create, inspect, and update page links and external destinations.
- [Actions](/pdf/cpp/actions/) - Configure document and page events, navigation buttons, and print, hide, and submit controls.
- [Bookmarks](/pdf/cpp/bookmarks/) - Add, read, update, expand, and delete document outline entries.
