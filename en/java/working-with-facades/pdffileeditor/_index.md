The `PdfFileEditor` facade works with PDF document structure and page layout. Use it to combine or split files, rearrange pages, and prepare booklet or N-Up output.

## What you can do with PdfFileEditor

Merge and split files, extract or rearrange pages, and change page layout. Content-level editing is covered separately in [PdfContentEditor](/pdf/java/pdfcontenteditor-class/).
